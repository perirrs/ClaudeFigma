How it works:
The extension tracks your browsing time and activity on platforms configured for your department (e.g., LinkedIn, Naukri, Sales Navigator, Monster, Dice, etc.).

When sync is enabled, a daily summary is sent to your team dashboard every 5 minutes. No raw browsing data leaves your machine — only aggregated productivity metrics.

Click the extension icon for today's summary. Click "Full Dashboard" for detailed history.
